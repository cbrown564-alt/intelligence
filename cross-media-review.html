The Shape of Intelligence · critical review Webpage + 48-second mobile film · 17 July 2026

Design · story · implementation

An extraordinary instrument with a split thesis.

The webpage says interfaces reveal and hide. The film says a mind can inhabit alien senses. The craft is already distinctive; the next leap is to make both media leave the same precise, unsettling idea in the audience’s head.

Needs revision before public promotion—not because it lacks wonder, but because the wonder currently outruns the claim.
Core opportunity

One particle. One claim. Two media: the film gives it an origin; the webpage gives the audience agency.

01 · Health

Good foundation. Important contradictions.

The system is accessible, technically disciplined, and recognisably authored. Its weakest points are hierarchy over time: what dominates, what disappears, and what the ending finally claims.

02 · Verdicts

Authored, not generic. But spectacle is carrying unfinished ideas.

Design

The full experience passes the AI-slop test. Petrona, the mineral palette, chapter-specific simulations, and confident black fields create a real science-gallery identity. Reduced motion slips toward the familiar serif-on-black editorial lane because the visual specimens become too sparse.

Story

The webpage has the better thesis. “Each form brings something into view and lets something else fall away” is specific and defensible. The film turns that into a linear ascent from sand to mind, then spends too little time earning the conceptual leap.

Implementation

The engineering is production-minded. Semantic fallbacks, lazy engines, native controls, focus states, error recovery, and a 66 KB gzip entry are excellent. The biggest confirmed runtime defect is reading-position loss when Motion is switched off on mobile.

The webpage refracts. The film ascends.

The first is a plural argument about interfaces. The second is a familiar technological genealogy. Their shared visual language disguises a genuine narrative disagreement.

05 · Recommendations

Protect the strangeness. Make the argument exact.

Webpage

01

Make reduced motion a first-class gallery

Author a static hero specimen and five genuinely different still compositions. Remove movement, not art direction.

02

Cut Forms from five proofs to three

Keep Music, Scent-adjacent flocking, and one pattern study. Move the remainder into an optional “Explore more forms” atlas.

03

Show the blind spot of every interface

Let each chapter reveal one new capability and one loss. The finale should feel earned because the trade-off has been experienced five times.

04

Preserve place when preferences change

After Motion or Detail changes chapter height, restore the active heading below the masthead. Add a 390px Playwright regression test for the toggle.

05

Translate implementation language

Rename “Detail full/lite” to “Graphics standard/low-power.” Replace “1,600 instances” and Greek indices with labels that describe the encounter.

06

Calm the conclusion

Suppress the persistent current during Vision and reduce the finale field. The final sentence—not 900 motes plus a second particle layer—should own the scene.

MP4

01

Rewrite the final three copy beats

Use “We translate that language into signal” → “Then build new interfaces with it” → “New senses bring a different machine into view.”

02

Keep one object alive through the coda

Let the chemical signal become a single light filament. It may pass through music, pattern, and city, but those worlds should not all remain on screen.

03

Move time from shoreline to thesis

Recover about two seconds from 00:00–00:05 and give the final reframing room to land. The ending currently introduces the biggest idea at the highest visual density.

04

Design for the player, not only the frame

Keep indispensable copy 10–12% from top and bottom. Supply an SRT and transcript even though text is burned in; silent meaning still needs an accessible channel.

05

Render a delivery family

Keep the 24 MB file as a master. Add a lighter 720×1280 MP4, a 12-second teaser, and a shorter GOP for better seeking.

06

Create an optional sound-on cut

Make matter audible: granular shore, laser ticks, electric transients, neural pulse, chemical hiss. Preserve the silent master; sound should deepen, never carry, meaning.

One particle becomes the baton.

The film follows its causal origin—shore, wafer, electricity, biology, chemistry—then ends as that same particle enters the webpage hero. The website becomes the laboratory where the audience touches its six transformations. Cinema supplies causality; interaction supplies agency.

Verified strengths

Lint, 7 unit/source tests, production build, bundle budget, visual checks, and video TypeScript checks passed. No horizontal overflow appeared at 1280, 640, 390, or 320px. Reduced motion imported no canvases. The live page had no console, network, or page errors in the evidence pass.

Confirmed defects and risks

The mobile Motion toggle can place the active H2 at 26.9px while the masthead ends at 64px. The desktop masthead title is below the project’s 44px target. The film is 24.0 MB at 4.01 Mbps with 8.33-second keyframe spacing, and ffprobe reports yuvj420p despite a yuv420p render request.

Test gap

The committed Scent screenshot contains copy that no longer matches src/content/chapters.ts, yet the broad 1% pixel threshold can still pass. Assert thesis-critical copy semantically; screenshots alone are too blunt for editorial truth.

Motion verdict

Needs revision before promotion. Subject-specific motion, deterministic film frames, and off-screen canvas gating are excellent. Final-object continuity, reduced-motion parity, player-safe copy, and preference continuity are not yet at the same level.
