# Product

## Purpose

The Shape of Intelligence is a visual essay that makes machine intelligence feel material, plural, and shaped by human choices. It argues through interaction as well as prose.

## Audience

- Curious general readers who know AI mainly through chat interfaces.
- Designers and technologists interested in alternative AI interfaces.
- Readers who may use a keyboard, screen reader, reduced motion, zoom, or a low-power mobile device.

## Core promise

A reader can understand the complete argument without WebGL, canvas motion, audio, or pointer interaction. When those enhancements are available and wanted, they deepen the same argument instead of replacing it.

## Primary journey

1. Meet the opening question and understand how to proceed.
2. Move through six clearly named chapters.
3. Follow one field of matter as it becomes force, signal, chemical, structure, and light.
4. Optionally disturb that field and interact with simulations that embody each chapter's idea.
5. Leave with the sense that intelligence changes with the form of the encounter.

## Non-goals

- Explaining how modern AI systems work in technical detail.
- Claiming that current AI is conscious or thinks like a person.
- Presenting experimental medical sensors as clinical diagnoses.
- Maximizing the number of effects, canvases, or chapters.

## Platform

Web. The quality bar is flagship: strong art direction, readable and operable at 200% zoom, responsive from 320 px upward, and stable on a mid-range mobile device.

## Success criteria

- The full hero message is visible on first paint and with motion disabled.
- Every chapter is reachable from desktop and mobile navigation.
- Reduced-motion mode imports no chapter animation engines.
- The persistent matter current remains subordinate to prose and chapter-specific imagery at desktop, tablet, and mobile sizes.
- Initial JavaScript remains below 150 KB gzip.
- Build, lint, source contracts, and semantic smoke tests pass from a clean install.
