# The Shape of Intelligence — companion film transcript

The film is silent. The text below includes the on-screen copy and the visual information needed to follow the causal handoffs.

**00:00–00:04 — “What is the shape of intelligence?”**

A dark shoreline fills the vertical frame. Individual grains lift from the beach and begin to travel upward.

**00:04–00:09 — “First, we shaped sand.”**

The same grains flow into a tilted silicon wafer. Their movement keeps the material handoff visible.

**00:09–00:14 — “Purified into silicon. Etched with light.”**

A fabrication gantry moves lasers across the wafer. Circuit paths appear where the scan points pass.

**00:14–00:18 — “Electricity activated the machine.”**

Current travels through the new circuit paths and branches away from the chip.

**00:18–00:25 — “But intelligence was already alive. In brains. In every cell.”**

The electrical branches become neural activity in a brain, then a complete DNA double helix.

**00:25–00:29 — “A swarm thinks in motion.”**

DNA releases into a winged insect swarm. Each insect responds to nearby motion rather than a visible leader.

**00:29–00:33 — “It speaks in chemicals.”**

The swarm follows chemical paths and carries molecular structures through the field toward a machine receptor.

**00:33–00:38 — “We translate that language into signal.”**

The molecules enter the receptor. An electrical signal leaves it as a continuous light filament.

**00:38–00:42 — “Then build new interfaces with it.”**

The filament passes through a ribbon, a repeated spiral, and a procedural city. These systems appear in turn rather than accumulating into one mind.

**00:43–00:48 — “New senses bring a different machine into view.”**

The surrounding systems dissolve. One horizontal filament and a single bright point remain, matching the opening composition of the webpage.
